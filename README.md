Opportunity One 🎯

Your Voice. Your Opportunities.

Opportunity One is an Alexa-powered assistant designed to help students discover scholarships, government schemes, internships, and other career-building opportunities through voice interaction.

🚀 Problem Statement

Many students miss valuable opportunities because information is scattered across multiple platforms and difficult to discover.

💡 Our Solution

Opportunity One makes opportunity discovery easier through a voice-enabled assistant that helps students find relevant information quickly and conveniently.

✨ Key Features

- 🎓 Scholarship discovery
- 🏛️ Government scheme information
- 💼 Internship opportunities
- 🗣️ Voice-based interaction using Amazon Alexa
- 🔎 Simplified access to opportunity-related information

🛠️ Technology Stack

- Amazon Alexa Skills Kit
- Python
- Alexa Developer Console

🎯 Target Audience

Students seeking financial assistance, government benefits, internships, and career development opportunities.

🌍 Vision

To make opportunities more accessible so that no student misses out simply because they didn't know where to look.

Project Name: Opportunity One
Category: Voice Technology / Education

📌 Future Enhancements

- Personalized opportunity recommendations
- Eligibility-based scholarship matching
- Application deadline reminders
- Multilingual voice support
